# Selected Work interaction

## Design plan

Five direct case-study links connect to a central reading preview. The mechanical-drawing vocabulary reflects the actual work: manufacturing, protective hardware, motion, and CAD. Names remain visible, with no project images on this overview. The branches show navigation choices, not parts of a physical assembly.

Palette: canvas #f7f8fc, ink #192942, muted #59687e, line #d2dae7, accent #264bdb; existing dark counterparts remain active. Type inherits the portfolio's current sans family, with large project titles and quiet supporting text. The center is left aligned; surrounding links follow a stable spatial composition. Desktop map height is 480px. Smaller screens use connected text entries and omit the redundant central preview.

Hover or keyboard focus selects a preview and highlights its connection. Each project opens directly on the first click; the center also offers a direct case-study link. The selection persists on pointer exit. No automatic orbiting, drag gesture, or additional selection step. Native links retain modifier-click behavior, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.

## Review before implementation

The previous numbered columns were compact but had little relationship to engineering. Remove arbitrary numbering and uniform ruled columns. Put the distinctive interaction in one place, keep the surrounding canvas open, and use real project outcomes rather than decorative statistics. Compact screens prioritize direct access over preserving a radial shape. Existing case-study media and experience styling are preserved.

References: Efren Cabebe's experience presentation, Rauno Freiberg's radial-menu study, and Anthropic's official frontend-design guidance. These inform the composition and interaction; the site uses ordinary navigation rather than a gesture-driven menu.

Accolades are intentionally deferred until the user finalizes this design.

## Section overview

The subsequent request adds a compact, initially collapsed index of all seven current sections. Opening a section mounts its complete content; minimizing it removes the content and stops its media. A second minimize control follows long sections and returns keyboard focus to the section toggle. Native hash links open their destination, including experience article links. Returning from a case study restores Selected Work and the originating project link.

The footer includes the user's exact product-name, logo, brand, and media ownership disclaimer. Certificates and recognition remain deferred until the project design is finalized by the user.
